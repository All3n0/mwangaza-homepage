# Geovani Capital Training homepage

Homepage for Geovani Capital Training, a Nairobi-based institute selling short (5–10 day) instructor-led professional courses to NGOs, public institutions and finance teams across Africa, in eight classroom cities and live online.

## Brand

- Name: Geovani Capital Training
- Palette (oklch tokens in `src/styles.css`):
  - Deep navy `#0B2545` (primary: buttons, links, brand)
  - Navy-deep `#081627` (body text, dark sections, footer)
  - Bright gold `#F5B700` (accent: key CTAs, date badges, highlights; fills and text on navy only)
  - Soft gold `#FFD466` (hover states, decorative details)
  - Gold text `#8A6400` (gold-coloured text on light backgrounds)
  - Paper `#FAFAF7`, mist `#EEF1F6`, stone `#5F6B7A`, line `#DDE3EC`
- Type: Fraunces (display), Manrope (UI and body)
- Content and figures live in `src/data/site.ts`; photos in `src/assets/photos/`.
